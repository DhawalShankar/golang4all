# Code of Conduct

GolangForAll — at meetups, in the WhatsApp community, and anywhere else people gather in this name — is meant to be a space where anyone can show up and learn, regardless of experience level.

## The short version

- Be respectful. Disagreement on technical points is normal and welcome; disrespect toward people isn't.
- No harassment of any kind — based on gender, experience level, background, or anything else.
- There are no "dumb questions" here. Beginners are welcome, and so is asking the same question twice.
- Keep discussions on-topic and constructive, especially in the WhatsApp group where things move fast.
- Self-promotion is fine in moderation — spamming the group with unrelated content isn't.

## If something goes wrong

If you experience or witness behavior that doesn't fit the above — at a meetup or in the WhatsApp group — reach out directly to **work.dshukla@gmail.com**. Reports are taken seriously and handled discreetly.

## Why this exists

The community only works if people feel safe participating in it. This page exists so that expectation is written down, not just assumed.
