# GolangForAll

> **Bringing Go developers together.**

GolangForAll is a community initiative for people who **learn, build, teach, and contribute with Go**.

Go already has its official home at [go.dev](https://go.dev). GolangForAll is not here to replace it.

We are here to bring **people** together.

## What We Do

### 🧑‍💻 Meetups

We organize Go meetups where developers can meet, learn, discuss, and share their experiences.

### 🎤 Talks & Workshops

A space for developers to share what they know—from Go fundamentals to production systems, cloud infrastructure, networking, distributed systems, and beyond.

### 🏗️ Build & Contribute

We encourage people to build projects, explore the Go ecosystem, and contribute to open source together.

### 📚 Learn

We maintain a simple collection of useful Go resources, guides, and community knowledge for developers at different stages of their journey.

### 🌍 Grow the Community

We want Go communities to exist wherever developers are building with Go.

Starting locally, growing across cities, and eventually connecting developers across India and beyond.

## Who Is It For?

**Everyone.**

Whether you are:

* writing your first Go program,
* moving to Go from another language,
* building production systems,
* contributing to open source,
* teaching Go,
* speaking at meetups, or
* simply curious about the language,

there is a place for you here.
