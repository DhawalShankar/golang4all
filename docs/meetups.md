# Meetups

GolangForAll meetups are informal get-togethers for Go developers to meet, learn, and share what they're working on.

## Where

Meetups currently happen mostly in the **NCR** and **Kanpur/Lucknow** region. As the community grows, we'd like this to expand to more cities.

## How to find out about the next one

We don't run a separate events calendar — meetup announcements go out on:

- 💬 [Our WhatsApp Channel](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VbDoRp9JP214yrRcCS2I)
- 🌐 This website

If you're not on WhatsApp yet, join the channel there first — that's where dates, venues, and topics get shared as soon as they're finalized.

## Want to host one in your city?

If you'd like to organize a GolangForAll meetup at your place — your city, your college, your office — we're happy to support it under the GolangForAll name.

Write to us at **work.dshukla@gmail.com** and we'll help you get it going.
