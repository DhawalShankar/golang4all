# Contribute

GolangForAll runs on people showing up and doing something — a talk, a project, a post, an afternoon spent helping someone debug their first goroutine. Here's how to plug in.

## Give a talk or workshop

Have something worth sharing — a production lesson, a deep-dive into a Go feature, a project walkthrough? We're always looking for speakers for meetups, any experience level.

Write to **work.dshukla@gmail.com** with a rough topic and we'll find a slot.

## Write for the blog

If you'd rather write than speak, the [blog](/blog) is open to community contributions — backend engineering notes, build logs, things you learned the hard way. Reach out on the WhatsApp group or by email and we'll get you set up.

## Build and open source

Working on a Go project, library, or tool? Share it in the WhatsApp group — feedback, contributors, or just visibility, whatever you're looking for.

## Host a meetup

Want to run a GolangForAll meetup in your own city? See the [Meetups](/docs/meetups) page.

## Just show up

Not everything here needs a contribution in the traditional sense. Coming to a meetup, asking a question in the group, helping someone else out — that's the community working the way it's meant to.
